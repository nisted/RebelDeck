Help

  

    
What is it? Virtual Stream Decks for Companion: in a window, on a Pi screen, or on any phone or tablet on your network.
      Based on Joseph Adams' ScreenDeck, given a good shake.

    
Companion side: nothing to install. RebelDeck uses Companion's Satellite API (Companion 3.5 or later).
      Each deck appears in Surfaces; give it a page there like any Stream Deck.

    
Phones and tablets: open  and pick a deck. On iPhone/iPad, Share → Add to Home Screen for full-screen.

    
Three ways in: the tray icon (quick stuff), the pop-out (connection and showing/hiding decks), and this page (everything).

    
Where settings live: ~/.config/rebeldeck/settings.json.
